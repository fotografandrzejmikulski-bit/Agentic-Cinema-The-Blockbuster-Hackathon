# 3-Minute Demo Script

**0:00–0:20 — Problem**
"A storm cancels Scene 42. The production team now has to reconcile location availability, cast windows, transport, overtime and budget exposure — under time pressure."

**0:20–0:50 — Input**
Enter: "A storm cancels Scene 42. Find the best recovery option within 48 hours and quantify the budget delta."

**0:50–1:35 — Agentic workflow**
Show the coordinator, parallel specialist roles, and the financial path grounded through ClickHouse MCP in the configured runtime. Emphasize that the agents solve independent subproblems and are reconciled into one decision package.

**1:35–2:15 — Decision cockpit**
Show ranked studio options, fit scores, estimated costs, budget delta, confidence and provenance.

**2:15–2:40 — Human gate**
Show the approval list: location, cost ceiling, overtime/contract changes and call-sheet release. Explain that the prototype does not silently mutate external systems.

**2:40–3:00 — Why it matters**
"StudioSync is not a movie generator. It is an operational control layer for the people who have to make the movie happen."
